FINDINGS:
  1. A deterministic retry on a machine-checkable form condition is the cheapest repair in the
     archive and it is total: 16/16, one turn, no prompt engineering, no best-of-two. When the
     condition is a fact about the artefact, the model fixes it on being told. That is worth keeping
     in the pipeline whatever it buys, because it costs one call and removes a whole failure class.
  2. It bought zero useful tests and five false alarms. Quantity of gates up 33%, quality down.
     Every repair this line has shipped (fence stutter, unclosed fence, stripdecl, goimports, unused
     silencing, and now the form check) fixes the ARTEFACT; none of them can fix what the artefact
     ASSERTS. The deterministic-repair line on this bench is CLOSED: everything a machine can check
     is now checked.
  3. The keep rule is the next thing to doubt, not the writer's form. Red-at-parent keeps a test that
     is red at parent, on gold, and on every candidate. 18 of 20 kept gates are of that kind. Any
     rule that could tell them apart needs a second observation the router does not have.
  4. The remaining distance is judgement, and judgement has a verifiable reward: RED ON PARENT AND
     GREEN ON GOLD are both toolchain facts, needing no LLM judge, and there are 195 mined tasks
     outside the eval set to train on. That is the first time on this campaign that training is the
     indicated next step rather than a nicer algorithm — earned by eliminating every alternative.
