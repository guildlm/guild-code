FINDINGS.
  1. A system prompt defined next to a call that cannot see it is a silent-wrong-input failure, the
     same family as the mlx_lm.server adapter bug of 2026-07-24: every arm looked served. The check
     that would have caught it on day one is the one used today: patch the transport and read what
     was sent.
  2. The writer's gap is judgement. Four independent routes to form have now been measured (form-retry,
     exemplar, compile-retry, the right prompt) and none moved USEFUL past 7 of 35. The false-alarm
     class (18) is the largest and is invisible to every run-time signal.
  3. The training arm's baseline is D1: 7/35 USEFUL, 18 false alarms, 25 kept. It is the first writer
     number in this line measured with the prompt that was registered.
