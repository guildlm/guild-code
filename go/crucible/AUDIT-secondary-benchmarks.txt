go_review_bench keyword-scoring robustness (2026-07-20)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
mlx_review_bench.py scores identify@1 as `any(keyword.lower() in review.lower())`
over ~5 concept keywords per task — one match is a pass. Ran the teeth lens on that:

  - ECHO-DEGENERATE 1/8: wrapped_error_eq lists `%w` as a keyword, and `%w` is in the
    prompt's code being reviewed. A model that merely echoes "%w" passes WITHOUT
    naming the real bug (== vs errors.Is on a wrapped sentinel).
  - GENERIC KEYWORDS that match by chance, not diagnosis: defer_in_loop scores on
    `loop` (any review of looping code says "loop"), double_close on `once`,
    defer_in_loop also on `leak`. `any()` means one incidental hit = a pass.

IMPACT: inflates ABSOLUTE identify@1, but the weakness applies to base and specialist
alike, so it does not bias the A/B direction (same shape as the contamination reading).
The review bench remains a rough, objective signal — just not a tight one.

RECOMMENDATION (do not mutate silently — it would strand any recorded review numbers):
drop the in-prompt/generic keywords (`%w`, `loop`, `once`, `leak`), or raise the bar
from `any()` to ">=2 distinct concept keywords", so a pass requires real diagnosis.
